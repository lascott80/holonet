A setting row is one option in Settings: a name on the left and its control on the right.

- Rows are at least `control` high inside a `card` container with `radius-lg`, divided by `line`.
- The name is `body`. An optional second line in `meta`, `ink-muted`, explains a consequence, not the control.
- Controls: a checkbox for on or off, a select for a list, a slider with its value for a range, and a segmented group for two to four exclusive choices.
- In a segmented group the chosen button is `raised` with an `accent` border.
- The row wraps on a narrow window: the control drops below the name.

Group related rows under a `heading`. Settings that can lose data or weaken security say so in the second line.

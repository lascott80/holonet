A tag is a short label attached to something else; it is never a button.

- **Group tag**: the group name in its group color, outlined, uppercase 11px. Used in pane headers and the session toolbar.
- **Neutral tag**: `ink-soft` text with a `line-strong` outline, for facts such as "Tailscale" or "Via Tailscale".
- **Keyboard hint**: the mono family in `ink-muted` with a `line-strong` outline.
- **Group square**: an 8px square in the group color before a host name, where a full tag would be too heavy.
- **Status text**: plain `meta` text; `warn` only when something needs attention.

Radius is `radius-xs`. Do not put more than two tags on one line.

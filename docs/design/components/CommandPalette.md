The command palette finds and opens anything in the app from the keyboard; it opens with Ctrl Shift P.

- It is a dialog 640px wide at most, placed in the upper third of the window over the 60% backdrop.
- The search field is 52px high with 16px text. An "Esc" hint on the right closes it.
- Results are grouped under `label` headings: Hosts, Snippets, Go to, Actions.
- Each result is `control` high. Hosts lead with their group square. The right side says what Enter will do, or shows the action's own shortcut.
- The highlighted result is `raised` and carries an "Enter" hint. Up and Down move the highlight.

Shortcuts shown in the palette use Ctrl Shift, so that Ctrl K, Ctrl T and Ctrl W still reach the shell.
